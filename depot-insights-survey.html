Survey Results 74 Respondents

Depot Insights — Smart Summary & AI Tools

Smart Summary awareness & AI-tool usage in the BackOffice — May–Jun 2026 across 4 countries

📌 Key Findings

👀
Low Smart Summary awareness: Only 36.5% of respondents have ever looked at the Smart Summary (27 of 74). The single biggest reason for not looking is simply not knowing it exists — 17 of 27 who gave a reason said they didn’t notice it was there.
⭐
Those who use it, like it: Among the 19 who rated usefulness, every single one found it useful — 47.4% “Very useful” and 52.6% “Somewhat useful.” No one rated it low.
🤝
Subcontractors are the power users: Subs are far more likely to have looked (43.9% vs GLS 27.3%) and far more enthusiastic — 8 of 12 rating subs said “Very useful,” while GLS staff were lukewarm (6 of 7 only “Somewhat”).
🤖
Solid AI adoption: 57.9% of the respondents use AI tools at work (22 of 38). Writing & rephrasing messages is the top use case (11 of 19), followed by analysing data, translating, and summarising. Subs who use AI lean “regularly”; GLS staff lean “occasionally.”
🚧
AI hesitancy is about exposure, not rejection: Of those explaining why they don’t use AI, the reasons split evenly between “haven’t had the chance to try” (6) and “don’t see the value” (6), with a few privacy / company-policy concerns.
