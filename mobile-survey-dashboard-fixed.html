📱 Mobile Backoffice Survey Dashboard

📌 High-Level Findings

  • Overall Adoption: The mobile backoffice tool has a moderate adoption rate of 56.1% among all survey respondents, with 169 out of 301 users utilizing the mobile version.
  • Adoption Varies by Country: Mobile adoption is highest in Austria (94.4%), while Canada has the lowest at 35.9%.
  • High Daily Usage Among Mobile Users: Daily usage is strong, averaging 74.2% overall. Netherlands leads with 87.5%.
  • Key Feature Usage: "Live Status" is most used (92.9%), followed by "Tour Performance" (62.4%) and "Usage & Analysis" (56.5%).
  • Primary Barrier to Adoption: The top reason for not using mobile is desktop preference (59.3%), followed by lack of awareness (22.0%).
  • Usability Pain Points: Some users (notably in Germany and Canada) mentioned issues like a confusing map, slow loading, and oversized UI elements.
